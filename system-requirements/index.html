Wanderburg System Requirements

Wanderburg system requirements and potato-PC positioning from Steam: what official copy says about hardware, controllers, and modest setups.

Specs are store-page positioning; verify live Steam requirements after each major patch.

Official positioning

Steam marketing for Wanderburg emphasizes that the game is optimized for modest hardware — the store pitch literally frames it as ready for a potato PC.

Exact Minimum / Recommended tables can change with EA patches. Always trust the live Steam system requirements panel over any wiki mirror.

Input and platform notes

Steam lists full support for mouse, keyboard, or controller. That matters if you planned to play on a living-room PC or handheld dock.

We did not find an official Steam Deck Verified badge during research. For Deck-specific notes, see the Steam Deck page on this wiki.

What this page will not invent

We will not fabricate FPS benchmarks, VRAM numbers, or unsupported console SKUs. If Steam later posts detailed tables, this page should quote those rows directly.

If your machine struggles, lower resolution first and keep an eye on EA performance hotfixes from Randwerk.
