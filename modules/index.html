Module priority by run phase

  1. 0–5 min: sustain / space — survive before stacking damage.
  2. 5–15 min: one AoE clear — cannons, arcane, or mines so swarms cannot pin you.
  3. After first boss fortress: specialize for boss damage or mobility based on what killed you.

Cannons

Steam lists cannons as core siege mounts for broadside-style pressure. Early runs: pick one reliable clear tool before stacking more barrels — mobility while turrets fire matters more than raw DPS in the demo.

Wizard / Arcane Towers

Arcane artillery and wizard towers provide alternate magical firepower. Good when swarms clump; pair with sustain so you can stand still long enough for beams to connect.

Explosives & Mines

Deployables for area denial while you reposition the castle. Use when fortress kiting is comfortable; avoid filling every slot with mines if you still die to projectiles.

Vehicles & Chassis

Between-run unlocks that change how the fortress handles and grows. Prioritize handling comfort if you keep dying to walls; handling upgrades pay off across all weapon packages.

Captains & Artifacts

Meta unlocks that widen build identity across runs (Steam description). Pick captains that reinforce your chosen package (artillery, sustain, mobility) instead of random novelty.
