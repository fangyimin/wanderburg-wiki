What Steam actually says

Steam’s progression blurb lists captains alongside modules, vehicles, and artifacts as between-run unlocks that widen strategic options.

Players searching wanderburg best captain usually want a ranked list. Before EA settles, we only document the role category — not invented captain names or fake tier scores.

How to use captains in practice

Treat a captain unlock as a build identity lever: once you have sustain and a clear damage package, a captain should reinforce that plan instead of fighting it.
