What “unlock” means in Wanderburg

Steam describes two layers: in-run modular siege gear that reshapes the current attempt, and between-run unlocks (modules, vehicles, artifacts, captains) that widen future strategies.

The free demo is meant to teach movement, sustain, and early build experiments — not to publish a full permanent checklist.

If a site sells “all item IDs / save editors,” skip it. This wiki will not mirror those lists.

Practical demo unlock goals

Goal A: Finish two short runs that only practice drifting and not dying — unlocks are secondary.

Goal B: When between-run options appear, pick tools that match what already kept you alive (heal / clear), not random legendaries.

Goal C: Note what the demo withholds (biomes, captains, late modules). That gap is intentional for EA.

Then move to the Beginner Guide and Best Early Build pages on this wiki.

Demo vs Early Access unlock expectations

Full Early Access (Sep 8, 2026) will expand content and balance over an estimated 6–12 month window.

Assume demo progress rules may differ from EA. Re-check Steam news at launch before grinding for permanent unlocks.

FAQ

How do I unlock everything in the demo? You probably cannot — and should not chase fake complete lists.

Do demo unlocks transfer to EA? Unconfirmed on research day; verify officially after launch.

Where is the official unlock table? Not published as a public exhaustive sheet in sources we used.
