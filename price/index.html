Is Wanderburg free?

The full Wanderburg Early Access build is a paid Steam product. Searches like is wanderburg free usually confuse the free demo with the main app.

The free Wanderburg Demo (Steam app 4268810) lets you try the fantasy before buying. Demo availability was described as ongoing after Next Fest — confirm on Steam.

Where to see the real price

Regional Steam pricing changes with sales and currency. This wiki will not hardcode a dollar amount that goes stale overnight.

Open the official store page for app 3624140 to see your local price, discounts, and edition notes.
