Wanderburg Best Early Modules

Wanderburg best early modules: heal and sustain first, then area clear, then boss tools. Honest priority order for demo and Early Access.

No official ranked module list on research day. Order below is a learning priority from Steam framing + demo-era tip articles — re-check after EA patches.

Priority ladder (early hours)

1) Sustain / heal modules (Carpenter / Zimmermann-style picks in tip articles) so you live long enough to practice driving.

2) Area-clear tools — cannons and on-hit explosion procs appear often in community demo guides.

3) Mobility comfort if you keep dying to positioning — handling is a skill gate (GameStar hands-on).

4) Boss / fortress damage once you can circle without melting.

Between runs, unlock modules that reinforce the same identity instead of random shiny options.

What “best” does not mean yet

There is no frozen post-EA ladder. Steam’s store copy celebrates wild build experiments each run.

We will not invent DPS tables, drop rates, or item IDs. Treat any undated “best mod” cheat list as outdated until you verify in your build.

After a major patch, prefer Steam news and Discord notes over this page’s examples — then we update the changelog.

FAQ

Is there one best early module? No. Start with whatever keeps you alive, then clear, then boss tools.

Do demo unlocks carry to EA? Assume progression rules can change — verify on Steam when EA launches.
